> **Developer Preview / Hardware Not Yet Verified:** X3 hardware and the deployed robot runtime have not been validated. Local tests do not establish device compatibility, physical behavior, or safe operation.

## Offline preparation and observation

`MouthController.prepare(ratio)` validates a finite normalized ratio between `0` and `1` and prepares data offline. `HeadController.prepare_rpy(roll, pitch, yaw)` validates finite numeric inputs in radians and prepares data offline. These checks do not establish mechanical limits or safe poses.

The controllers' `inspect(node)` methods examine the graph using a caller-owned ROS node; they do not command movement. `EyeController.inspect(node)` reports a candidate graph observation, not eye position.

## Physical control

Manual mouth and head writes raise `MotionUnavailableError`; eye motion has no manual SDK interface. Before any future physical control, device-specific limits, arbitration, stop behavior, and supervised hardware acceptance must be established. Do not infer safety from an absent competing node.
