> **Developer Preview / Hardware Not Yet Verified:** S100 ROS integration and physical behavior have not been verified on a target device. Local tests and profile labels do not establish hardware compatibility.

Warmcore's product specification identifies vision and tracking as Pro capabilities; Ultra includes these and adds facial touch. These product descriptions do not identify the board or prove a given unit's interfaces.

| Capability | Product specification | SDK preview and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vision and tracking | Pro and Ultra | Image and follow-state subscriptions implemented; actual camera and tracking remain unverified on target hardware. |
| Facial touch | Ultra addition | Offline normalization implemented; live event path not established in the inspected baseline. |
| RF433 | Availability depends on unit | Receiver subscriptions implemented; device presence and mappings require verification. |

Confirm SKU, board, firmware and peripherals with an authorized operator. An SDK profile is a label, not hardware identification.
