> **Developer Preview / Hardware Not Yet Verified:** Local tests do not establish S100 device behavior or compatibility. Live ROS, firmware, and hardware acceptance require device-specific evidence.

## Missing ROS interfaces

Confirm the ROS environment, interpreter, namespace, domain, middleware, and authorized vendor overlay with the operator. Interface discovery is a snapshot and does not guarantee compatibility or delivery.

## No touch or vision events

Check the actual SKU and installed hardware. Confirm the running graph and publisher before concluding a feature is available. Dedicated Ultra facial-touch live event publication remains unverified; do not treat RF433 events as proof of facial-touch delivery.

## Preview evidence

The S100 preview records offline test and loopback coverage. Hardware, live ROS endpoints, firmware assignments and device-specific behavior require acceptance evidence. When requesting help, include sanitized software versions and errors; remove addresses, credentials, device identifiers and personal or conversation data.
